JFX-JOURNAL Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 October 2026

JFX-JOURNAL (“JFX”, “we”, “us”) is a trading journal web and mobile app operated by Phemelo Prince Moatshe in South Africa. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and your rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address and profile picture, when you sign up with email or with Google.
  • Google sign-in: we receive only your name, email address and profile picture. We do not access your Gmail, Drive, Calendar or other Google data.
  • Trade data: trades, balances, positions and account details imported from MT4/MT5 (through the Desktop Bridge or broker VPS sync), from cTrader, or entered manually.
  • Journal content: notes, tags, screenshots, strategies and rules, backtests, goals and milestones, performance reports, and psychology notes about your mindset, emotions and discipline.
  • Connections and keys: broker access tokens and JFX Developer API keys. These are stored encrypted and never shown in full after creation.
  • Usage data: log data, IP address, device and browser type, app version and basic feature analytics.

2. How we use it

  • To create, secure and sign in to your account.
  • To sync, store and search your trade history.
  • To produce your analytics, trade reviews, summaries and sentiment analysis, and to check trades against your rules.
  • To track your goals and send milestone alerts.
  • To run the Developer API, including enforcing your portfolio locks and risk rules on orders.
  • To fix problems, prevent abuse and improve JFX.
  • To contact you about your account, security or important changes.

We do not sell your personal information or use your trade data for advertising.

3. Psychology and journal notes

Your psychology notes and journal entries are private to your account. We use them only to give you insights, and never share them for any other purpose.

4. Google user data

JFX’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to sign you in, is never sold, and is never used for advertising or to train AI models.

5. Who we share it with

Only service providers that help run JFX:

  • Supabase (database, authentication, hosting)
  • Brokers and platforms you connect, such as cTrader (Spotware) and your MT4/MT5 broker or VPS, to sync accounts and place orders you or your authorised agents request
  • AI model providers that process the trade reviews and questions you request
  • Hosting and analytics providers

We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

6. International transfers

Some providers process data outside South Africa. We use only providers that offer adequate protection, as POPIA requires.

7. How long we keep it

While your account is active. After you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your information within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

8. Security

We use encryption in transit, encrypted storage for tokens and keys, access controls and row-level security. Keep your password, Desktop Bridge and API keys private.

9. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your information, object to processing, or withdraw consent at any time. You can disconnect brokers and revoke API keys in Settings, and remove Google access in your Google Account. You may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.

10. Children

JFX is for people aged 18 and older.

11. Changes

We will post updates here with a new effective date, and notify you of significant changes.
