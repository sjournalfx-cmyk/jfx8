Limits set before you trade

Set portfolio-level limits in advance. Trading Locks apply them when you place orders through JFX.

  • Allowed-pairs whitelist, maximum trades per day and minimum or maximum lot size
  • Hard limits checked by the JFX order ticket
  • Disabling or loosening a lock starts a 24-hour cooling period with a visible countdown
  • Closing a position and editing SL/TP stay available
  • In-app, browser and Android notifications, including blocked-trade alerts
Trading Locks are risk tools, not guarantees. They do not replace broker-side risk controls or your own judgment.

Mentor and Research modes

JFX Mentor can work with your journal while JFX Research stays outside it, and the boundary is visible before you ask a question.

  • JFX Mentor reviews the journal context you ask about: trades, notes, daily bias, rules and performance patterns
  • JFX Research explores general market concepts, setups and risk ideas without access to your private trading data
  • Voice input and transcription for hands-free questions
  • Save useful answers as notebook notes
  • Daily usage credits keep the assistant available during beta
AI responses can be incomplete or wrong. JFX is not a financial adviser, and AI output is not trading advice.

FAQ

Questions traders ask

Straight answers about access, connections and data.

Is JFX Journal free to use?

Yes. The free Journaler plan gives you 50 trades a month, one notebook note, manual trade entry and the core analytics. Pro and Premium show as FREE BETA in the app. Pro lists a 500-trade monthly limit and Premium lists unlimited trades. Limits and features can change during the beta.

Which brokers and platforms can I connect?

JFX connects to cTrader through OAuth, with separate demo and live environments. The JFX Desktop Bridge syncs MT5 account data and closed trades. You can also import trade history from a CSV file or enter trades by hand.

Can I place trades from JFX?

Yes, on connected cTrader accounts. The order ticket handles market, limit, stop and stop-limit orders, closes positions and changes SL/TP. Trading Locks can block new orders. Closing a position and editing SL/TP stay available.

Does JFX work on mobile?

Yes. JFX runs in desktop and mobile browsers, and the Android app uses the Capacitor native shell. The app connects to the same production backend. You can sign in with email and password or Google.

Is my trading data private?

Your journal is tied to your Supabase account, with row-level security enforced on the database. cTrader broker tokens stay on the server, API keys can be revoked, and Research mode does not receive private journal data. Mentor mode reads the journal context you ask it to review. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Can I try JFX without a broker account?

Yes. You can log trades by hand and open a reversible, read-only demo journal with sample trades and notes. You can also connect a cTrader demo account for testing.

Does JFX provide financial advice?

No. JFX is a journaling and analysis tool. It is not a broker or financial adviser, and nothing in the app is financial advice. Statistics, backtests and AI responses can be wrong. Past performance does not predict future results, and trading forex and CFDs carries a high risk of loss.

Is there a Developer API?

Yes. You can create personal API keys with scopes for trades, notes, bridge data and your profile. The API reads journal metrics, logs trades, and works with cTrader orders and candles through documented endpoints. You can revoke your API keys at any time.
