Trading Locks

Pre-commit to the rules

Set portfolio-level limits before emotion gets a vote. Trading Locks enforce the boundaries you choose when you place orders through JFX.

  • Allowed-pairs whitelist, maximum trades per day and minimum/maximum lot size
  • Hard limits checked by the JFX order ticket
  • Disabling or loosening a lock enters a 24-hour cooling period with a visible countdown
  • Closing a position and editing SL/TP always remain available
  • In-app, browser and Android notifications, including blocked-trade alerts
Trading Locks are risk tools, not guarantees. They do not replace broker-side risk controls or your own judgment.

Questions traders ask.

Straight answers about access, connections and data.

Is JFX Journal free to use?

Yes. The free Journaler tier includes 50 trades per month, one notebook note, manual trade entry and core analytics. The Pro and Premium tiers are marked FREE BETA in the app; Pro shows a 500-trade monthly limit and Premium shows unlimited trades. Plan limits and features can change during beta.

Which brokers and platforms can I connect?

JFX connects to cTrader through OAuth, with separate demo and live environments. MT5 account data and closed trades can sync through the JFX Desktop Bridge, and trade history can also be imported from CSV. Manual entry is always available.

Can I place trades from JFX?

Yes, for connected cTrader accounts. The order ticket supports market, limit, stop and stop-limit orders, closing positions and changing SL/TP. Trading Locks can block new orders, but closing a position and editing SL/TP always remain available.

Does JFX work on mobile?

Yes. JFX runs in desktop and mobile browsers, and an Android app is available via the Capacitor native shell. The native app connects to the same production backend; email/password and Google sign-in are supported.

Is my trading data private?

Your journal is tied to your Supabase account with row-level security. cTrader broker tokens are handled server-side, API keys can be revoked, and Research mode does not receive private journal data. Mentor mode uses the journal context you ask it to review. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Can I try JFX without a broker account?

Yes. You can log trades manually and explore the app with a reversible, read-only demo journal containing sample trades and notes. A cTrader demo account can also be connected for testing.

Does JFX provide financial advice?

No. JFX is a journaling and analysis tool, not a broker or financial adviser. Statistics, backtests and AI responses can be wrong, and past performance does not predict future results. Trading forex and CFDs carries a high risk of loss.

Is there a Developer API?

Yes. You can create personal API keys with scopes for trades, notes, bridge data and your profile. The API can read journal metrics, log trades, and work with cTrader orders and candles through documented endpoints. Keys can be revoked at any time.
